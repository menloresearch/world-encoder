## 1. Why state-as-query (evidence already verified, REDESIGN_RESEARCH_20260729.md)

- AdapTac (strongest force-fusion result in corpus): privileged modality forms the cross-attn
  **queries** over other sensors' K/V — 93% vs 73% vision-only. BUT its own ablation shows the
  wiring alone loses to vision-only; the **future-prediction aux is the driver**. Query design
  and objective are ONE package.
- MCR (only frozen-encoder pretrain with real policy wins) is built on state-action grounding —
  its dynamics-alignment term is the largest ablation factor.
- Manipulation-centricity (attention on EE + task objects) predicts policy success (R≈0.93);
  state-conditioned queries are a mechanism that aims attention exactly there.
- Known failure mode: **proprio shortcut** (DiT-Block Policy needed per-dim state dropout) — on
  the encoder side this means queries extracting only state-predictable content. Mitigation +
  detection is a first-class design axis, not an afterthought.

## 3. Fast-iteration protocol (screen cheap, confirm once)

Funnel — each stage kills before the next spends:

1. **Free offline pre-filter (~minutes, no policy run):** action-readout ladder (clean episode
   split), state R², RankMe, SeeSE3 dSE3@0.25s, + (pending agent-04 recipes) Burns CLS-attention
   Jaccard and MCR manipulation-centricity on the encoder ckpt. Probes are NECESSARY-not-
   sufficient here (v0.3 lesson: they invert as sufficiency signals) — used only as sanity
   floors + shortcut detectors (state-zeroing sensitivity).
2. **Encoder pretrain:** ~2-4h on the robocasa cache (E3 harness), co-residable 2-3/GPU.
3. **Policy screen (1 seed):** standard recipe, `checkpoint_every=10`; **gate at ep10-20** once
   the calibration data (ep10/20/30/40/50 curves now accumulating from un-pool + insertion arms)
   shows early rank predicts ep50 rank. Kill discipline per [[early-kill-discipline]]: gates
   pre-registered BEFORE numbers are seen.
4. **Fast eval for screens:** reduced-step diffusion inference (DDPM-16 / DDIM-16, validation
   run vs the known 66% square ckpt IN FLIGHT) + 50 rollouts. 25-rollout screens only for
   ≪-band kills (binomial noise at p≈0.5: ±2σ ≈ ±14pp @25, ±10pp @50 — 25 can kill, not rank).
5. **Confirm (top-2 only):** ep50+ep100, DDPM-100, 50 rollouts, 1 seed.
6. **THE BIG RUN (winner only):** 2-3 seeds, ep100+, standard eval, + transfer tasks
   (tool_hang/coffee if force is in the design). Nothing else ever gets multi-seed compute.

Throughput math: screen ≈ 2-4.5h train (ep10-20) + ~15-25min eval; 20GB arms co-reside 2/GPU on
the A6000s → ~10-14 concurrent screens box-wide when v0.3 runs clear ⇒ ~8-12 verdicts/day vs
current ~2-4. Encoder-side diagnostics (gate value off zero, state-zeroing delta) read straight
from ckpts at ep10 — a dead arm dies without a single rollout.

## 5. Pre-registered gates (to fill AT DESIGN FREEZE, before any v0.4 number is seen)

- Screen kill: ep10/ep20 fast-eval below <calibrated band vs dense curve> → kill. Gate math
  from 04: 25 rollouts kill at ≤7/25 vs p₁=0.5 (false-kill 2.2%, exact binomial); 50-rollout
  screens can't see <12-13pp; frame screens as SELECTION with a kill band, never significance
  tests (certifying 10pp costs ~280 rollouts/arm).
- **Shortcut kill (from 02, at ep10 + ep50):** D1 null-state/null-vision action-MSE sweep on
  ~100 held-out demos (intact / dataset-mean state / permuted state / background-color images):
  ΔMSE_vision ≈ 0 while ΔMSE_state large → shortcutted → KILL regardless of success. D2
  state→z ridge/MLP probe: conditioned-token R²≳0.9 AND z→object-pose at the state-only floor
  → encoder collapse → kill. Continuous monitors: T1 val-loss INVERSION (v0.4 arm val loss
  >20-30% better than v0.3 baseline early = red flag, not a win), T2 state-pathway grad norm
  dominating vision epochs 1-10 or gate saturating in first epochs.
- **Aux-health gate (from 03, at ep10 ckpt, before any rollout):** (1) zeroing VISION barely
  moves the aux loss → arm is shortcutted → kill; (2) chaseability — aux loss must visibly
  descend (unchaseable target was the E4/torque-aux failure signature).
- Promotion: within/above dense band at ep20 → ep50 confirm; top-2 at ep50 → ep100; single
  winner → big run. Certification (winners only): 100-200 rollouts × 2 seeds, Wilson/UMA
  bounds, disjoint one-sided CIs at 0.975 (STEP or truncated SPRT for sequential stopping:
  20pp bands decide in ~30 rollouts avg at α=β≈0.05).
- **Fast-eval caveat (04 + Consistency Policy data):** reduced-step eval collapses on
  precision tasks — DDiM-15 on Tool Hang: 0.79→0.14. Our DDPM-16 validation (0.64 vs 0.66)
  was on SQUARE only; calibrate per task family before trusting fast eval anywhere else;
  never mix samplers across arms inside one comparison. tool_hang tonight is judged on
