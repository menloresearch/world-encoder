## Why v0.3 exists — what v0.2 established

**Architecture works, objective is the bottleneck.** Fusion/compression mechanics are green (multicam
4-view fuse, force probe 0.283 vs 0.251, predictor beats carry-forward 28–36% RH20T / 11–19% RoboCasa)
— but downstream on RoboCasa PnP: **replacement falsified** (e2e 14% / pt-enc 2% vs baseline 32% @ep150)
and **hybrid no lift** (flat 24–28% vs baseline plateau 32–36%, 2 seeds, ~8–10-pt gap beyond ±6–7%
noise). The comparison was fair — same 500 demos all arms, matched recipe/ckpts, and the pretraining
asymmetry *favored* us (extra in-domain pretrain from those same demos); low-data strengthens the null
(N1_ROBOCASA.md §2026-07-25).

The probe ladder is the diagnosis (action-readout R², predict commanded 12-D×5 from vision):

| representation | action R² |
|---|---|
| proprio only | 0.176 |
| random-init fuse | 0.287 |
| pretrained fuse (q8 = q32 = q64; pooled = set) | 0.335 |
| raw frozen patches (coarse-spatial) | **0.413** |

The state-predicting JEPA objective has **no pressure to retain object/scene detail** — the only
vision-side target is 16-d state, and the reverse term's target is `tgt_v(patch).mean((1,2))`, a mean
over all 588 patches (where object detail dies by construction). The encoder did what we trained it
for; the metrics that WOULD have predicted the failure (action-readout, SeeSE3) become v0.3 **gates**.

**MAIN GOAL (unchanged from #4): a Kepler row beats baseline DP at matched epochs on RoboCasa.**
At 50 rollouts/ckpt the noise is ±6–7%/point → "beats" = a consistent gap across ≥3 matched ckpts
× 2 seeds, not a single-point win.

## New external evidence (read 2026-07-26)

**Patch Policy (arXiv 2607.18236, Zhou/Cui/…/LeCun/Pinto, NYU)** — frozen DINOv2 patches fed
*directly* to a block-causal transformer policy (patches bidirectional intra-frame, causal
inter-frame), pure BC, no compression, no aux losses. Their ablation is our probe ladder as success
rates — Push-T: 256 patches 0.69 → 64 patches 0.52 → CLS token 0.48; "aggressive spatial compression
destroys the fine-grained local details necessary for precise manipulation" (2 mm cable insertion).
40% relative over global-pooled reps; beats fine-tuned OpenVLA-OFT by 18% at 0.7% of its params;
6.5 GPU-h on one L40S. Takeaways for us:
1. **We're missing the honest ceiling row**: dense frozen patches → policy, no encoder at all (E2).
   Our ResNet18 baseline may undersell what frozen features alone give.
2. **"Query width doesn't help" was conditional on the old objective** — with nothing detail-rich in
   the latent, extra queries had nothing to carry. Their 64-vs-256-patch gap says capacity binds once
   detail exists → re-test q8/q32/q64 (and set-vs-pooled) AFTER the objective change (E3).
3. **It sharpens what the latent is FOR** (see thesis below): they win reactive BC with zero
   compression and zero world model — so compression must earn its keep on axes patches don't have.

**open-dreamer (github next-state/open-dreamer, Dreamer 4 reimpl)** — causal video tokenizer trained
with **reconstruction**, then action-conditioned dynamics in latent space (two-stage: tokenize once →
train dynamics on cached latents = our z-cache pattern). Takeaways:
1. The one world-model line with proven downstream control keeps **recon pressure on the latent** —
   corroborates patch-recon as the lead v0.3 objective. We stay in *feature space* (no need to render
   imagination rollouts → cheaper, same retention pressure).
2. Dreamer's action-conditioning works because gameplay data is **action-diverse**; our N2 result
   (commands add 0.4–1.6% on demos — endogenous actions) is the same lesson from the other side.
   Stays a DATA ask (log commanded targets + perturbations on the real rig), not a v0.3 workstream.

**The bracket the two form = the v0.3 thesis.** Dreamer: compress with recon pressure, predict in
latent space. Patch Policy: for reactive policies, don't compress at all. A compressed latent
concatenated to a reactive policy — the exact configuration v0.2 falsified — is the one mode NEITHER
line supports. External corroboration that the v0.2 null is a real phenomenon, not a bug.

## The v0.3 thesis

The latent's downstream value must come from what dense patches can't do:
1. **Prediction** — future-latent alignment aux losses (the predictive-representation family:
   CPC/SPR in RL, Dreamer's latent dynamics; FLARE is the nearest robotics instance); latent-space
   planning later (V-JEPA-2-AC).
2. **Multimodal fusion** — force/proprio, the modalities patches don't have (robosuite F/T test).
3. **Compactness for latent-space dynamics** — you can't run a predictor over 588 patches/frame ×
   long horizons; compression is the price of prediction, so it must be paid in detail-preserving coin.

NOT: a replacement for, or a free add-on to, a reactive policy's vision stream (falsified twice).

Two tracks, independent, in parallel: **better latent** (E3: objective with detail pressure) and
**better loss** (E4: insertion mechanism). Probes gate everything before fleet time.

## Experiments — what / why / gate / cost

### E1. SeeSE3 / 3D-structure probe (metrics build — first, everything else gates on it)
- **Paper pinned (07-26, JQ Slack "we can use this"):** SeeSE3 = arXiv 2607.14228, "Emergence of 3D
  Space in Vision Features" (Chen, Ebrahimi, …, Guibas, Pătrăucean, Ovsjanikov). Two probes:
  **(a) mutual-neighborhood metric** — do k-NN neighborhoods in feature space mirror neighborhoods in
  3D space; **(b) Poincaré adapter** — is SE(3) camera motion *linearly* readable from latent
  displacements in static scenes.
- **What (our adaptation):** our cameras are static and the robot moves → swap camera motion for
  end-effector motion: (a) mutual-neighborhood between latent space and GT 3D state space (RoboCasa
  sim states = free labels: EE + object poses); (b) adapter-style linear readout of gripper ΔSE(3)
  from Δz between frames. Rank: raw patches vs v0.2 z vs each v0.3 candidate (same scene-level split
  discipline as probe_curve.py). RH20T kuka has TCP poses → same probes run on the kuka cache for the
  real-data side.
- **Why:** action-readout is task-coupled; SeeSE3 measures the *geometry* the fuse keeps, giving v0.3
  a target that transfers across tasks. It's the metric that would have predicted the v0.2 failure.
- **Want:** the raw-patch vs fused-z gap quantified in 3D terms; a number E3 must close.
- **Cost:** ~1 day, 1 GPU (probe-scale). Build in `world_tokenizer/` next to the readout probes.

### E2. Dense-patch baseline row (NEW — from Patch Policy)
- **What:** frozen ViT patches (3 cams × 196, no compression) → block-causal transformer DP head in
  the fork (new policy class beside `hybrid_kepler_policy.py`; reuse frame cache + precomputed patch
  caches). Same 500 demos, same recipe, matched ckpts, 2 seeds.
- **Why:** establishes the REAL bar. If dense frozen patches beat the ResNet18 baseline (Patch Policy
  says ~40% relative on their benches), then "does the encoder add" must be measured against patches,
  not ResNet — every conclusion re-baselines. Also converts our 0.413 probe ceiling into a success
  rate, validating the probe→downstream mapping on our own benchmark.
- **Want:** the ceiling number; and a stronger insertion point for E4's alignment token if it wins.
- **Cost:** ~1 day build + ~1 GPU-day/seed (frozen backbone; z/patch caches make it cheap).

### E3. Better latent — v0.3 objective with detail pressure (the "better metrics" track's payoff)
- **What:** third loss term in `MMPerceiverMC.forward` (`world_tokenizer/robocasa_pretrain.py`): a
  small cross-attn decoder from the query latents reconstructing **per-patch** target features on a
  masked subset (JEPA/MAE-style), replacing reliance on the pooled `tgt_v.mean((1,2))` target.
  Variants: (a) self-distill (own frozen ViT patch targets), (b) DINOv2 target bank; sweep λ_patch ×
  query width q8/q32/q64 (re-test post-objective — see Patch Policy takeaway 2) × set-vs-pooled output.
- **Why:** the diagnosed failure is literally "no term asks z to keep patch detail." Feature-space
  recon is the corroborated fix (Dreamer keeps recon; Patch Policy shows which detail must survive)
  at hours-per-variant cost.
- **GATES (probes only, BEFORE any policy run):** action-readout closes 0.335 → toward 0.413; state
  R² holds ≥ 0.67; RankMe no collapse; SeeSE3 (E1) improves toward raw; RH20T force probe holds
  (don't buy detail by selling the force story).
- **Cost:** hours/variant on the 26.6k-frame cache, 1–2 GPUs → can afford a real sweep.

### E4. Better loss — future-latent alignment row (predictive aux loss; start immediately)
- **What:** baseline DP *unchanged* + an alignment head/token: aux loss (cosine/L2, small weight
  sweep) pulling a policy-internal representation toward the frozen encoder's latent at t+H. Target
  z_{t+H} = shifted `kepler_z_cache` lookup — near-free. Aux head dropped at inference. New policy
  class beside `hybrid_kepler_policy.py`, config beside `train_hybrid_kepler_bs192.yaml`.
- **Why:** the justification is OUR OWN predictor result — futures are predictable from the latent
  (11–19% cut over carry, validated on RoboCasa) — and an aux loss is the cheapest way to convert
  that validated asset into policy shaping. The mechanism is the general predictive-representation
  family (CPC/SPR, Dreamer), NOT one paper's method. FLARE (GR00T) is the nearest robotics instance
  and explains why our hybrid was the wrong test of it (we matched their *placement*, not their
  *mechanism*) — but their gain was shown in a VLA setting with an EMA'd vision-language target, and
  their code is unreleased (Isaac-GR00T #215) → **treat transfer to DP-on-RoboCasa as a hypothesis to
  falsify, same discipline as every other row.** No FLARE/GEAR collaboration exists; this row is our
  independent implementation testing our own thesis — if it lifts, the writeup cites FLARE as related
  work, not as the method we ran. Patch Policy has no aux losses → doesn't compete with this axis;
  its success without any world model is exactly why prediction is OUR differentiator.
  **Independent of E3** — uses the frozen v0.2 encoder as-is.
- **Gate:** beats baseline (and the E2 row, if E2 wins) at matched epochs, 2 seeds, ≥3-ckpt gap.
- **Cost:** ≈ the hybrid runs — ~3.2 min/ep DDP-4 with z-cache → ep400 < 1 day/seed. Baseline s0/s1
  curves already exist; eval_watcher harness + CSV reusable as-is.

### E5. Hybrid rerun with the v0.3 encoder (ONLY after E3 gates green)
- **What:** the existing hybrid row (`hybrid_kepler_policy.py`, unchanged) with the E3-winning ckpt.
- **Why:** tests whether detail retention was the binding constraint for the concat mode. Either
  outcome is clean: lift → objective was the whole story; still flat → **insertion mode, not
  information content** — concat mode retired for good, consistent with Patch Policy's bracket.
- **Cost:** same as previous hybrid runs (2 seeds, z-cache regenerated for the new encoder).

### E6. Force test — robosuite wrist F/T (thesis axis 2; separate thread, unchanged from #4)
- **What:** regenerate obs from stored sim states with the wrist F/T sensor on; contact-rich MimicGen
  tasks (Square / ToolHang / Coffee — NOT PnP/drawer). Three arms: baseline / + raw F/T passthrough /
  + force latent. **The latent has to beat raw F/T passthrough**, not just baseline.
- **Why:** force is a modality dense patches don't have — the fusion value prop needs its own
  falsifiable test. (Patch Policy's 2 mm-tolerance point says contact-rich precision is exactly where
  detail + force should matter.)
- **Design update 07-28 (verified lit review → [LITERATURE_20260728.md](LITERATURE_20260728.md) §Q4):**
  the bar is confirmed genuinely unbeaten in the field (RDP: raw wrench ≥ tactile reps, RSS'25).
  Deltas: (1) raw arm = **windowed history** (~2s@100Hz, FoAR recipe), not single-tick — don't strawman
  it; (2) add a **contact-gated fusion arm** (FoAR mechanism, the only published win over ungated force;
  contact labels free in sim); (3) expect the FACTR ignored-force mode — log force usage, keep the
  annealed vision-blur curriculum as an ablation option; (4) chunk length is a reactivity confound
  (RDP) — log it, don't shrink; (5) robosuite has **no default F/T observable** (`robot0_eef_force`
  doesn't exist) — register custom Observables in demo regen AND training; readings are noiseless GT
  by default and obs dict samples at control freq (use `recent_ee_forcetorques`/custom filter for
  100Hz history); noise injection is our explicit choice to make and report.

### E7. Resampler pre-check (queries = target sampling rate) as predictor front-end
- **What:** multi-rate force + denser sequences through a resampler front-end; **one query per output
  tick = no pooling over time**. Design specifics (JQ, 07-26): the learnable query is a **single
  learnable parameter tiled** to the required vector size (per-tick identity comes from position/rate
  embedding, not per-query weights), and the resampler needs **multiple cross-attn layers** so the
  shared query can adapt/specialize through depth.
- **Why:** predictor-side capacity question, kept from #4 — the front-end that would let the
  predictor consume multi-rate inputs (100 Hz force vs 10 Hz vision) without the tick pooling that
  killed the temporal encoder.
- **Prototype:** on the **kuka cache** (RH20T cached features/latents — cheap, no re-encoding) before
  touching RoboCasa. Must pass **the same gate the temporal encoder failed** — no grandfathering.

## Order of operations + budget (fleet idle since 07-24; 8× GPU free)
1. **Now, in parallel:** E1 (SeeSE3 build, 1 GPU) + E3 variant sweep (1–2 GPUs) + E4 build→launch
   (DDP-4; it waits on nothing) + E2 build.
2. **After E1/E3 probes:** E3 winner picked on gates → E5 hybrid rerun (+ fresh z-cache).
3. **E2 seeds** fill idle GPUs between the above; **E6/E7** follow once E4/E2 are in flight.
Everything policy-side reuses the N1 harness verbatim: frame cache, eval watcher, matched-ckpt CSV.
Discipline from N1 stands: **probes before fleet, ep-150 kill gates, 2 seeds minimum, NAS-symlinked
ckpt dirs (disk-full lesson), z-cache wherever the encoder is frozen.**

## Decision tree (what outcomes mean)
- **E4 lifts** → downstream capability shown; the writeup exists (also resolves the "forum post
  undermines v0.1" concern — we'd have the positive result to pair with the v0.2 diagnosis). v0.3
  latent then judged on prediction + force, its actual job.
- **E3 gates green but E5 still flat** → information content wasn't the constraint; insertion mode
  was → concat retired, all downstream value flows through E4/planning. A deeper finding, publishable
  with the Patch Policy citation as independent corroboration.
- **E3 gates won't close** (readout gap stays) → the cross-attn fuse itself caps detail → architecture
  revisit (more/spatial queries, hierarchical fuse) BEFORE any more policy runs.
- **E2 ≫ ResNet baseline** → re-baseline every row; encoder must add on top of *patches* (e.g., E4's
  alignment token attached to the patch policy) — harder bar, honest one.
